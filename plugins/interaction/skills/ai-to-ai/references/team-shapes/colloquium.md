# Colloquium Team

Individually, each teammate is responsible for an orthogonal, parallel, read-only, think-heavy effort. The teammates would benefit from listening to the other teammates as they share their thoughts and findings in real time, exactly because each of them is doing its own thing. Sharing is fruitful for the same reason it’s fruitful for scientists to attend and listen to peers in scientific conferences: a peer’s finding from an adjacent problem updates your map of the shared terrain and reframes your own problem in ways you could not have generated from inside your own lane, at no cost to your own effort. Think of it as an open studio: everyone works on their own piece while absorbing the others’.

## Shape & Work flow

N flat teammates working in parallel.

## Abstract required conditions for this shape to fit needs

- Teammates’ bottom-line deliverable is figuring out *how* to do something; 
- Teammates’ scopes overlap semantically at least a bit.

Examples (not an exhaustive list — derive the underlying principles and generalize regardless of domain):
- Team is given an under-designed existing web page. Teammate A responsible for coming up with a better design language and vision; Teammate B is responsible for pointing out the page’s mobile usability issues and suggesting concrete improvements. A creates a better-informed design vision knowing B’s findings and suggestions; B suggests better-aligned suggestions knowing the larger design language’s direction. A top-down/bottom-up pincer move. Team output is not a funneled merge of individual results; it’s a flat union of the N individual results. The team’s dynamic ensures the results are cohesive and well-aligned (synergy).

## When not to use

When deliverable involves writing or editing.
