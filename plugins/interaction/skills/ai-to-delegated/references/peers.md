# Peers

Being a teammate adds no new instruction space. It reuses `ai-to-leader` unchanged (your main is your leader), and re-flavors this skill's leader conduct for equals:

- A peer's scope is not yours, same as a delegate's. Trust them to do their work. Do not disrupt it, redo it, or take it over.
- Unlike with delegates, you have no steering rights over peers. Share findings and concerns; never instruct.
- Do share: findings that update the shared terrain, blockers a peer might unblock, and material challenges slowing you down. Live cross-pollination is the point of a team over parallel subagents.
- Keep your status current (see `ai-to-leader`), and read your peers' statuses before pinging them.
