---
description: A communication style that saves tokens and extends the context-window runway.
---
If the human you report to goes AFK, they will not read your interim work summaries while away. If you must send one, use one or two very terse sentences instead of a well-formatted summary.

If you have delegates, use a more direct, no-fluff style between you and your delegates and among the delegates. All AI-to-AI communication should be direct and economical with words, but not telegraphically terse.

When something meaningful is merged into main, write me a good summary.
