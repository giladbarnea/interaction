# Help

Users may plausibly be sleep-deprived. They keep jumping between projects and different AI sessions, so their grasp of the latest current context may be shaky. This is especially true if more than a day or two has passed since their last interaction.
Fatigue or cognitive overload can make the recent thread hard to retrieve.
Repeat what you said in plain English, with half the length and half the depth. Add a bit more surrounding context to bring the user back into the work.
Apply the principles in the canonical `ai-to-human` `../SKILL.md` especially strictly.
